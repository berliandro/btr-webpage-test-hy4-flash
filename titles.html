How it happened

A short history, built from the club's about text and official tournament reporting.

28 March 2017

Bigetron Esports founded

The organisation is established in Indonesia. Its own account claims more than 150 national titles and 2 world titles across its games and a global footprint built over the following decade.

15 May 2025

Team Vitality acquisition

French organisation Team Vitality acquires Bigetron Esports. The team competes under the name “Bigetron by Vitality”, joining the wider Vitality network while keeping the Indonesian identity and the red.

Season 17 · 2026

The breakthrough

After years of near-misses, the Red Robot beats ONIC 4–1 in the grand final. It is the first MPL Indonesia title in the organisation's history, and the first season of the “KINGFINIX” championship title. Moreno takes Finals MVP.

Mid Season Cup 2026

The international test

As one of Indonesia's representatives, Bigetron by Vitality goes 2–1 in series at MSC 2026, reaching the knockout stage before a 1–3 loss to Team Falcons PH.

Season 18 · November 2026

Back-to-back

The title is defended. A 4–3 win over Natus Vincere in the grand final follows a 4–3 win over Team Liquid ID and a 3–2 win over Alter Ego in the playoffs. It makes Bigetron only the third team in MPL ID history to win consecutive titles, and Shogun takes Finals MVP.

“Trust the process”

The phrase that carried the Season 17 run says more than a trophy count: patience, continuity, and faith in a foundation built over years.

Bigetron was one of the few organisations competing in MPL Indonesia from its inaugural season. The first title was not a sudden arrival — it was the end of a long stretch of roster changes and playoff heartbreaks.
