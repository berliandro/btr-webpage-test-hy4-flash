# Surface brief — index.html

Scope and visitor mode: the roster home is an Experience surface — the work leads from the first viewport; the interface recedes. The same world governs the shop, media, titles and matches pages.

Audience and job: Bigetron by Vitality / MPL Indonesia fans, stat-focused followers and merch shoppers. They come to read the current lineup, sense the team's identity and legacy, and find official and fan-made merchandise.

Proof and content: real team, roster, records and history; shop products with outbound redirect links to official and fan-made listings. No invented commercial claims; illustrative content must be labelled synthetic.

Constraints: unofficial fan hub, never imply endorsement; client-pinned red theme; greenfield static multi-page site.

Chosen direction and memorable moment: The Drop Lookbook — a full-bleed trophy photograph under a red wash, headed "OUR CHAMPIONS". The five transparent player cutouts are fused one-per-card with their data into an off-grid brutalist selector rack in the roster section, and the page closes with a stitched official / fan-made divider.

Unresolved decisions: concrete product catalogue; real roster data and imagery; whether the recommended Community / Fan Zone page is included.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A fan-built team hub where the roster is a seasonal lookbook, not a card grid. It refuses the near-black neon esports template: Bigetron red is the garment-dyed ground, not an accent.

OWN-WORLD: Garment-dyed team red (#B21018) as the dominant ground, ink black (#171412), warm off-white paper (#F3EDE2), one brass accent (#D8A24A). Heavy condensed display type, stacked and warped, for names; a clean grotesk for UI labels. Component language: hangtags, stitched rules, woven labels, editorial portrait plates, paper-grain surfaces.

STORY: Visitors understand this is the fan-built home of Bigetron by Vitality. They see the season's lineup first, feel the team's identity and legacy, and know the shop — with its clear official / fan-made split — is one gesture away.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed 1920×1080 photograph of the team lifting the MPL Indonesia Season 18 trophy, washed to team red so the celebration stays on-world. A dark-to-red gradient on the left carries giant stacked condensed "OUR / CHAMPIONS" in cream and light brass, with a small "BIGETRON BY VITALITY · MPL INDONESIA" identity line and a slowly pulsing brass rule below it. Two actions sit below the copy: brass "ENTER THE SHOP" and ghost "MEET THE FIVE", which scrolls to the roster selector. A rotated hangtag reads "DROP 01 — CHAMPIONS · SEASON 18". The photo breathes on a slow loop; the photo, copy and hangtag sit on separate depth planes that respond to pointer movement. The facts rail and the start of the roster section are visible at the fold. Unresolved decisions: none for this viewport.
