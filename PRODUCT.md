## Platform

## Stack

delegated: user asked for a recommendation. Recommended static multi-page HTML/CSS/JS with no build step — a content-first, image-rich fan hub with many sections and outbound links, easiest to host (e.g. GitHub Pages/Netlify) and maintain. Recorded so later work knows the choice was offered.

## Users

Primary visitor is "all of the above" — one audience with overlapping jobs:
- Bigetron by Vitality / MPL Indonesia fans who want roster, stats, records, and history in one place.
- Stat-focused esports followers and scouts checking results, titles, and match history.
- Shoppers looking for team merchandise, both official and fan-made.

Situation: casual web browsing on a team they follow; the site is a destination to stay informed and to discover/buy merch.

## Product Purpose

An unofficial fan hub that consolidates the current Bigetron by Vitality team — roster, stats, records, history, media, titles, and matches — plus a shop directory of official and fan-made merchandise. Each shop product carries outbound redirect links (official and/or fan-made) to the real product. Success: a single go-to destination where a fan can learn everything about the team and find merch without hunting across sites.

## Positioning

Unofficial, community-driven fan hub — not sanctioned by the team or MPL. Its distinguishing mechanism is combining a full team-information core with a merch directory that clearly separates official from fan-made products behind a thin section divider, with a redirect link per product. The product catalog is to be gathered automatically with agentic-AI help from online sources.

## Operating Context

Web browsing. Shop products link out to external stores: official team/MPL/Mobile Legends storefronts and fan-made product listings. Source material to consult for facts and assets: Mobile Legends: Bang Bang official websites, Liquipedia, and Bigetron's official website.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Multi-page static site with these named pages: roster (home), shop, media, titles, matches, plus at least one additional page to be recommended and confirmed later.
- Shop: two clearly separated sections — official merchandise, then a thin divider, then community/fan-made merchandise. Each product shows redirect link(s) to its source listing.
- Content scope: current team info, stats, records, history, plus media/titles/matches.
- Data and imagery must be sourced from MLBB official sites, Liquipedia, and Bigetron's official website; do not present invented data as official fact.
- Undecided: exact additional page(s), the concrete product catalog, and whether any runtime data fetching is needed versus static content.

## Brand Commitments

- Team: "Bigetron by Vitality" (Bigetron / Bigetron Esports), an MPL Indonesia team.
- Red color theme — user-stated binding visual constraint, recorded here without further visual direction.
- Must be clearly framed as an unofficial fan hub; never imply official endorsement.
- Team names, logos, player photos, and brand assets are tied to the real organization and must be treated with that in mind.

2. Be transparent about being unofficial; never imply official sanction or endorsement.
