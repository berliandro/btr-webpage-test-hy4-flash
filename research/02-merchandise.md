## Summary of flags for the implementing agent

1. **Replace all four `shop.bigetron.gg` links now** — the domain is gone. The current site's first four official cards (Omnia jersey, 2018 jacket, black tee, grey tee/wristband catalogue) either 404 at DNS level or point to a non-resolving host.
2. **Keep Shopee `https://shopee.co.id/btrgear` prominent** — bigetron.gg's own "SHOP NOW" button goes there, and @btrgear calls it the official e-commerce store.
3. **Use local image files** for all Bigetron social imagery; the signed Instagram/Facebook URLs expire. ESL and vitality.gg CDN URLs are stable but can be cached locally safely.
4. **Do not assert a wristband or the grey tee is currently sold** — no official source found.
5. **Do not describe Vitality/ESL items as Bigetron/MPL gear** — they are global Team Vitality products via licensed partners.
6. **Price volatility:** Shopee/TikTok drops, "limited pre-order" runs and ESL sale prices change constantly; the site's policy of omitting prices and linking out is correct.
